* EN 301 549 guidelines for Accessibility requirements
    * _Any_ interface for _anything_ computer controlled
    * The latest version has been published, updating various requirements and unifying and expanding the coverage across more countries.
    * https://etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/301500_301599/301549/04.01.00_30/en_301549v040100va.pdf
    * TLDR: Websites, mobile apps, documents, etc. should now meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA or above
    * Reminder that conformance is required for any service operating in most countries it covers, and strongly advised in the UK for any service (only required for public services still currently).
